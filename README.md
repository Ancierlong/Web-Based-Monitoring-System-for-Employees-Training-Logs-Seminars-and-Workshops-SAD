System Analysis and Design with Project Management:
Web Based Monitoring System for Employees’ Training
Logs, Seminars and Workshops

- Employee Information Registry (CRUD):
A centralized administrative database to Create, Read, Update, and Archive/Delete employee profiles.
Tracks vital employment details, department affiliations, and professional roles within the university.

Training & Professional Development Ledger:
Logs completed seminars, workshops, and training programs for each employee.
Supports document uploads (such as digital certificates of participation or attendance) to verify completed training.

Automated Email Reminder System:
Dispatches automated email alerts to employees regarding upcoming seminars, registration deadlines, or mandatory training renewals.
Sends automated notifications to department heads or admins when compliance thresholds or annual training hours are met (or pending).

Analytical Dashboard & Reporting:
A monitoring interface for university administrators to generate compliance reports, track overall professional development progress across departments, and identify skills gaps.
